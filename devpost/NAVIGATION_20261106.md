# Chapter navigation — 6 November 2026

Open `runtime/TwoOaks/TwoOaks.esproj` in Lens Studio 5.23.2. Wait for initial asset processing and compilation to finish. In Preview, choose Interactive and No Simulation for mouse control, then set Device Type Override to SPECS in Preview settings; this enables the SPECS APIs. SPECS 27 simulation is a separate hand-input view.

The bottom utility row contains Home, Reset view, Chapters, Evidence and Settings. Chapters opens a numbered nine-section menu, with the current section marked. Choose any section directly. The selected DNA variant, prediction and experimental result remain saved when changing sections. Return closes the menu at the point where it was opened. Opening Chapters, Evidence or Settings pauses the lesson; only one overlay is visible at once.

Home starts again and restores station placement and specimen orientations. Reset view restores specimen orientations. In Preview, left-click selects and left-drag rotates a specimen; right-drag moves the editor camera. In Settings, left-drag the Move station handle to reposition the lesson. Hide laboratory removes the room. Native SIK interactions remain available for the SPECS route.

At Test prediction, choose Trichomes or No trichomes before Add working GL1 becomes available. The comparison is between a new experimental leaf and the unchanged control. After the result, Try again clears just the experiment so another prediction can be tested. Both the result and prediction are retained when visiting other sections. Replay at the conclusion starts a fresh lesson.

The DNA letter caption follows the selected reference or S92F variant, including after returning from another chapter. The lesson remains nine sections and the film remains 170 seconds.

Previous controls-revision and viewer-revision bundles are retained. This bundle is prepared locally; publication and contest submission are separate owner actions.
